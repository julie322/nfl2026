🏈 NFL Play Viewer

Select the data folder to begin.

Watch every pass play, frame by frame

Click Open data folder and choose this project's data folder (the one containing games.csv and tracking/). Your browser may ask to confirm the "upload"; the files stay on your machine.

Then pick a game, click a play, and press ▶. Red pass rushers try to reach the gold quarterback; blue blockers try to stop them.

    Keys: space = play/pause, ←/→ = step a frame, ↑/↓ = previous/next play. Arrows show where each player is moving (longer = faster); the white tick on each dot shows which way he's facing.
